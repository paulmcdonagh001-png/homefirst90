Privacy — beta version

HomeFirst90 is currently an early public beta.

  • No user account is required.
  • The current calculator does not send your household answers to a HomeFirst90 database.
  • If you press Save this home, the saved plan is stored locally in your browser using localStorage on that device.
  • No advertising or affiliate tracking is active in the current MVP.
  • No third-party analytics tracker has been added yet.

If analytics, accounts, payments or affiliate links are added later, this page will be updated before those features go live.

You can remove the locally saved home by clearing site data for HomeFirst90 in your browser.
