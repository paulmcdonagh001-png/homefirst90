# HomeFirst90 Growth Agent — INITIAL APPROVAL QUEUE

This seed queue exists to train the agent. Nothing here is published automatically.

## Candidate 1 — Moving-out cash
Hook:
Moving into your first place? Your deposit and first month's rent aren't the full number.

Destination:
https://homefirst90.com/moving-out-budget-calculator.html?src={{channel}}

Best channels:
Facebook, Instagram carousel, Pinterest, YouTube Short.

Core content:
Show the five buckets that need cash before/around moving day:
- upfront housing;
- moving/transport;
- setup essentials;
- first household bills;
- emergency cash left afterwards.

Visual:
Simple vertical "The number isn't just the deposit" graphic with five stacked cost buckets.
