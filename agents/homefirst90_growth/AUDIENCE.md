# HomeFirst90 Growth Agent — AUDIENCE

HomeFirst90 is life-stage driven rather than age driven.

Core audiences:
1. First-time renters moving out of the family home.
2. First-time buyers preparing for their first property.
3. Couples moving into a first shared home.
4. Renters changing property and trying to budget properly.
5. People who have just collected the keys and feel overwhelmed by what needs doing.
6. Parents/family members helping someone move out for the first time.

Current geographical focus:
United Kingdom first, because current costs, bills and site language are UK-oriented.

Common problems:
- "How much money do I actually need before I move?"
- "What will my monthly bills be?"
- "How much will furnishing the place cost?"
- "What do I genuinely need to buy first?"
- "What am I forgetting?"
- "What do I need to do before moving day?"
- "I've got the keys — what do I do first?"

Audience mindset:
- often stressed;
- spending a lot of money;
- worried about missing hidden costs;
- looking for practical answers rather than lifestyle inspiration alone;
- likely to use a phone;
- sceptical of generic SEO articles and affiliate lists;
- willing to use a calculator if it produces an immediate personalised answer.

What earns attention:
- a specific money/problem hook;
- a simple checklist;
- a surprising forgotten cost;
- a concrete example budget;
- "what to buy first" prioritisation;
- a before/after keys timeline;
- screenshots that show the tool doing useful work.

What turns people off:
- corporate marketing language;
- vague "make moving easier" slogans;
- fake urgency;
- overly polished influencer content;
- unrealistic perfect-home imagery;
- long sales pitches before delivering value;
- content that looks like an estate-agent advert.

The agent should make the user think:
"That is exactly the problem I'm trying to work out."
