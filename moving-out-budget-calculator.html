England only. This is a planning calculator, not legal or financial advice. Current government guidance says a tenancy deposit is generally capped at five weeks' rent where annual rent is under £50,000 and six weeks from £50,000 to £100,000. For most new assured periodic tenancies, rent in advance is generally limited to one month after the tenancy is signed. A holding deposit can be up to one week's rent and is normally refunded or credited, so this calculator does not automatically add it again. Check your own agreement and the latest official guidance: GOV.UK tenant fees guidance.
