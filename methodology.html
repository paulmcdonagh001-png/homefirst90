HomeFirst90

How the setup estimate works

HomeFirst90 is a planning tool, not a live shopping quote. It estimates the cost of getting a home usefully set up from a representative basket of mainstream UK prices, then adjusts for household size, furnishing level, second-hand buying and what you already own.

Last calibration: 24 September 2026. Retail prices change constantly, so individual products may be cheaper or more expensive than the planning figure shown.

What the three spending levels mean

  • Keep it basic: entry-level products, selective second-hand buying and fewer non-essential upgrades.
  • Comfortable / sensible: mainstream new products without deliberately choosing premium brands.
  • Spend more for quality: more generous allowance for durability, finish and higher-spec choices.

Why the result is still only an estimate

Your town, sales, delivery charges, room sizes, brand preferences and whether appliances are already included can move the real total substantially. The purpose of HomeFirst90 is to answer a more useful early question: roughly how much cash do I need, what is urgent, and what can wait?
