What should the average cost to furnish a house include?

A useful furnishing budget needs to cover more than a sofa, beds and a dining table. A first setup can also include mattresses, curtains or blinds, lighting, storage and — if the property does not already provide them — major appliances. HomeFirst90 therefore treats the number as a whole-home setup cost, then lets you remove anything you already own or the property already includes.

If you are comparing averages online, check what is actually included. A furniture-only figure can look much lower than the cash needed to make the home properly usable.

Why a single average is not enough

The real number changes with household size, what the property already includes, what you already own, the quality level you choose and whether you are comfortable buying some furniture second-hand.
