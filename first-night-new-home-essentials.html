The practical first-night list

Sleep

  • Mattress or somewhere genuinely comfortable to sleep
  • Bedding, pillows and a clean change of clothes
  • Phone chargers within reach rather than buried in a moving box

Bathroom

  • Toilet roll, towels, soap and basic toiletries
  • Any regular medication kept with you, not in the removal van

Kitchen

  • Kettle, mugs, drinking water and an easy first meal
  • A small amount of cookware/crockery rather than unpacking the entire kitchen
  • Bin bags, washing-up liquid and kitchen roll

Cleaning and practical jobs

  • Basic cleaning supplies
  • Scissors or a box cutter for packaging
  • A small tool kit, torch and spare batteries
  • Extension lead if sockets are not where you expected them

Safety and access

  • All keys together and clearly labelled
  • Know where the stopcock and consumer unit/fuse box are
  • Check smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms where relevant
Rule: if an item does not help you sleep, wash, eat, charge something, stay safe or clean up on night one, it probably does not need to be in the first-night box.

Buying everything before the move can backfire

Room sizes, storage and what the property already contains are often clearer after you get the keys. Use a prioritised list so the essentials are protected and lower-priority spending can wait until you have lived in the space.
